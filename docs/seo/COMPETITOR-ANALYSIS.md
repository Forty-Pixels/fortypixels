# Competitor Analysis — Forty Pixels

**Created:** 2026-07-29
**Method:** Live SERP research (July 2026) + live-site checks. No DataForSEO/Ahrefs access in this session, so authority figures are **estimates, not measured** — flagged where that matters.

## SERP reality for LK agency terms

The first observation matters more than any individual competitor: **directories, not agencies, own page one.**

For "best web design agency Colombo Sri Lanka" and adjacent terms, the top results are [TechBehemoths](https://techbehemoths.com/companies/web-design/colombo), [Sortlist](https://www.sortlist.com/web-design/colombo-lk), [Clutch](https://clutch.co/lk/web-designers), [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/agency/website-design-development/lk/colombo), and [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/companies/web-development-agency/sri-lanka). Individual agency sites appear below them.

**Implication:** the fastest route to visibility for LK commercial terms is not outranking these directories — it's being ranked *inside* them. They are also the sources AI assistants lean on when asked "who are the best web design agencies in Colombo," which makes them a GEO play as much as an SEO one. Forty Pixels appears in none of them.

## Direct competitors

### Tier 1 — the upmarket benchmark

**Sphiria Digital Studio** ([sphiriadigital.com](https://sphiriadigital.com/)) — the closest analogue to where Forty Pixels says it wants to be, and the sharpest competitive threat.

- Colombo HQ, positioned as "Premium Web Design & Web Development Agency Sri Lanka" — owns the word *premium* in its title tag
- Explicitly markets **Next.js and React** — the same stack Forty Pixels builds on, but says so where buyers can search it
- Markets "SEO & AEO optimization built in" — already competing on answer-engine visibility
- Claims 700+ clients across LK, USA, UK, Australia, Canada — the exact "local core + global" posture chosen here
- Trustpilot presence, Behance portfolio, RocketReach company/team profiles → strong entity footprint
- Does **not** publish pricing

**What they beat Forty Pixels on:** entity footprint, named team, breadth of proof, keyword-explicit positioning, directory presence.
**What Forty Pixels can beat them on:** craft and taste (the site itself is genuinely better executed), pricing transparency, and depth-over-breadth case studies. "700+ clients" is a volume claim; five deeply documented builds with real metrics is a quality claim, and quality claims win five-figure deals.

### Tier 2 — established LK generalists

| Agency | Position | Notable |

These win on **domain age and accumulated links**, not on craft. Forty Pixels will not out-authority them in 12 months and should not try. Beat them on specificity and modernity.

### Tier 3 — the commodity floor (avoid competing here)

[webslk](https://www.webslk.com/web-design-pricing/) (one-pagers from LKR 18,500), [Webivox](https://www.webivox.lk/web-design-prices/), [Lexiata](https://lexiata.lk/website-price-in-sri-lanka/), [Nexdin](https://nexdin.com/web-design-price/), [360Digital](https://360digital.lk/web-design-development/pricing/), [Kushan Dreamworks](https://kushandreamworks.com/), [web-dev.lk](https://www.web-dev.lk/web-design-price-list-sri-lanka/) (LKR 80k/130k/150k tiers).

**This tier has an SEO strategy Forty Pixels is accidentally executing.** They publish price-list pages and rank for "web design price Sri Lanka," "cheap website Sri Lanka," "web design packages." Those keywords convert — into LKR 40,000 projects.

Forty Pixels' `/pricing` page, with LKR 30,000–170,000 tiers, is structurally the same asset. If it gets optimized and ranks, it will win exactly this traffic. **The pricing page should be repositioned before it is amplified.**

Worth noting: web-dev.lk explicitly markets the arbitrage — "a comparable website costs $2,000–5,000 in Australia or the UK, while Sri Lankan rates of Rs 80,000–150,000 ($250–470) deliver the same quality." That's the frame the whole LK market is trapped in, and it's the frame the upmarket goal requires escaping. Selling into USD markets at USD-adjacent prices means competing on quality signals, not geography.

**Brand defense (urgent, see strategy doc):**
`forty pixels`, `fortypixels`, `forty pixels agency`, `forty pixels sri lanka` — currently contested by Forty Eight Pixels, 44 Pixels, Fifty Pixels, and FORTYPIXEL (Kiel). Google's own summary conflated the brand with an unrelated freelancer marketplace.

## Recommended competitive posture

Do not fight Tier 2 on authority or Tier 3 on price. Position as **the small studio that builds the thing properly** — the deliberate counter-positioning to both "700+ clients" volume shops and LKR 18,500 template mills.

Three assets carry that positioning in search: deeply documented case studies with real metrics, named humans with credentials, and technical-specificity content. All three are also exactly what AI search engines cite. That alignment is the plan.
